Match the coating to the floor

Start with compatibility—not a universal coating

Rockhard T900 is a solvent-based, flexible clear topcoat made for resilient flooring. The manufacturer identifies vinyl, linoleum, VCT, sheet vinyl and vinyl plank among the surfaces it is designed to protect. That does not make it a coating for every floor.

JW first checks the existing material, prior wax or finish, visible wear and cleaning history. Concrete, hardwood, ceramic tile and laminate belong in a different service conversation. A compatible floor also needs to be clean, dry and sound enough for the selected system.

Protection with honest expectations

Scratch-resistant is not scratch-proof

The T900 technical data describes resistance to wear, abrasion, scratching, impact, scuffs, acid etching and common food and beverage stains. It can support a simpler maintenance approach than repeated strip-and-wax cycles, but no clear coat eliminates damage or routine care.

Because the finish dries clear, marks, color variation and repairs beneath it may stay visible. A small test area can help confirm compatibility and appearance before the full floor proceeds.

Before scheduling: Product availability, finish selection and project-specific requirements are confirmed first. No fixed price, cure time or warranty is promised until the surface and selected product are confirmed. Review the manufacturer’s T900 technical data sheet ↗.

Questions before an estimate

Useful answers before the site visit.

These answers summarize the product information and planning approach; the floor still needs a project-specific assessment.

Which floors can be considered for T900?

The manufacturer lists flexible vinyl floors and other soft substrates, including linoleum, VCT, sheet vinyl and vinyl plank. JW still confirms the exact surface and its condition.

Is Rockhard T900 scratch-proof?

No. It is designed to resist wear, abrasion and scratching, but traffic, grit, furniture and maintenance still affect the finish.

Will it hide existing marks?

No. It dries clear, so scratches, stains, color changes and repairs may remain visible. Preparation and a test area help set expectations.

Which sheen is available?

The manufacturer describes gloss, satin and matte versions. The chosen finish and current product supply are confirmed before scheduling.
