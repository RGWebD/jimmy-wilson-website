Questions before an estimate

Useful answers before the site visit.

These answers are planning guidance, not a diagnosis of a surface that has not been inspected.

Is luxury vinyl plank waterproof?

Product claims vary. Many planks resist water at the surface, but water can still reach walls, seams or the structure below. Review the exact product literature for its limits.

Can LVP go over existing flooring?

Sometimes. The existing surface must be stable, suitable and within the selected product’s requirements. Height and transitions also have to work.

Do planks need time in the home before installation?

Acclimation rules vary by manufacturer and product. JW follows the written requirements for the material selected for the project.

Can you install one continuous look through several rooms?

Often, but layout, room dimensions and product limits may require transitions. JW will review the connected floor plan before defining the installation.
