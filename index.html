Before you call

Questions homeowners ask first.

If your project falls outside these basics, call and describe what you are looking at. A surface problem is easier to price after it is seen.

What kinds of flooring do you install?+

LVP and LVT, porcelain and ceramic tile, engineered hardwood, solid hardwood and laminate, plus rental-property and fast move-in flooring.

Can JW handle paint and flooring on the same project?+

Yes. Painting, pressure washing, drywall work, flooring and concrete coatings can be scoped together so you are not coordinating separate crews.

What epoxy and coating work is available?+

Garage flake and polyaspartic systems, commercial epoxy, pool-deck and lanai coatings, epoxy repair and recoating, plus clear coats and sealers.

Do you repair damaged floors?+

JW handles water-damaged floor replacement, buckled or cupped floor repairs, popped or cracked tile and time-sensitive move-in work.

Who receives the 10% discount?+

Seniors, military members and veterans, law enforcement, firefighters and emergency service members. Mention the appreciation discount when you request your estimate.
