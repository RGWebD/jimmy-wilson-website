Questions before an estimate

Useful answers before the site visit.

These answers are planning guidance, not a diagnosis of a surface that has not been inspected.

Can a stained garage slab be coated?

It may be possible, but contamination must be evaluated and treated as part of preparation. The inspection determines whether additional removal or remediation is needed.

Do you offer decorative flake finishes?

JW can discuss decorative flake and other coating looks. Availability and the final system are confirmed during the estimate.

Will the coating make the floor slippery?

Texture and traction can be discussed when selecting the finish. Wet conditions, footwear and cleaning practices still matter on any coated surface.

When can vehicles return to the garage?

Vehicle return depends on the specific coating products and conditions during curing. JW will give project-specific guidance after the system is chosen.
