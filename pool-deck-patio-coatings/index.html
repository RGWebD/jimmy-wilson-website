Scope the work before choosing shortcuts

Outdoor concrete needs more than a new color

Chalky coatings, stains, cracks and loose areas can affect the next finish. Pressure washing may be part of preparation, but cleaning alone does not correct an unsound layer or ongoing movement. JW reviews accessible conditions and lists repairs or removal separately from the finish coat.

Choose for the way the space is used

Traction and surface comfort belong in the selection

A finish that looks smooth in a sample may behave differently when wet or exposed to direct sun. Texture, color and the way the area will be used should be discussed together. No coating makes wet surfaces risk-free, so sensible maintenance and use remain important after installation.

No fixed price, cure time, warranty or performance claim is made on this page. Those details belong in the written scope for the selected system and actual site conditions.

Questions before an estimate

Useful answers before the site visit.

These answers are planning guidance, not a diagnosis of a surface that has not been inspected.

Can a pool deck coating be slip-proof?

No surface should be described as risk-free when wet. Texture options can be discussed, but drainage, maintenance, footwear and use still matter.

Can you coat over an old deck finish?

Possibly, if it is sound and compatible. Loose, chalky or incompatible areas may need removal or additional preparation.

Will coating hide every crack?

Repairs may reduce visible cracks, but concrete can continue to move. JW will explain likely appearance and limitations based on the inspected area.

Can screens and pool equipment remain in place?

Many fixed elements remain, but access and protection needs vary. The estimate should specify what must be moved and what JW will protect.
