Scope the work before choosing shortcuts

The repair depends on why the floor moved

Cutting down one raised edge without understanding the surrounding installation can leave the cause in place. Visible symptoms, recent water events, perimeter clearance and changes across the room all inform the repair discussion. Conditions outside finish-floor work may require another qualified trade before JW replaces or resets material.

Questions before an estimate

Useful answers before the site visit.

These answers are planning guidance, not a diagnosis of a surface that has not been inspected.

What causes a floor to buckle?

Possible causes include moisture, restricted expansion, installation issues or movement below the finish. An inspection is needed before choosing a repair.

Can a buckled plank simply be pushed back down?

That may not address the cause and can damage the locking edges. The surrounding floor and available movement space should be reviewed first.

Will a replacement plank match?

A match depends on available stock, production variation, age and wear. Spare material from the original installation is often the best candidate when it is compatible.

What if the floor is still wet?

Active leaks and unresolved moisture should be handled before finish-floor repair. JW can define the flooring scope after the underlying condition is addressed.
