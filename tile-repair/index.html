Scope the work before choosing shortcuts

One damaged tile can have several causes

Impact damage looks different from repeating cracks, widespread hollow sounds or tiles that lift together. The repair plan should reflect what is visible rather than assuming every failure is cosmetic. JW explains where a spot repair is reasonable and where a larger area needs to be opened or referred for further evaluation.

Choose for the way the space is used

Matching material is part of the repair

Even the same product name can vary between production runs, and older tile may no longer be available. Spare tile from the original job is useful when its size and condition match. When an exact match is not possible, JW can discuss a deliberate replacement boundary instead of scattering visibly different pieces through the room.

No fixed price, cure time, warranty or performance claim is made on this page. Those details belong in the written scope for the selected system and actual site conditions.

Questions before an estimate

Useful answers before the site visit.

These answers are planning guidance, not a diagnosis of a surface that has not been inspected.

Can you replace a single cracked tile?

Often, when matching material is available and the surrounding installation is stable. The crack pattern should still be reviewed before treating it as isolated.

What does a hollow-sounding tile mean?

It can indicate incomplete bond or a void, but sound alone does not define the full condition. The extent and any movement should be assessed.

Can cracked grout be repaired without removing tile?

Sometimes, but recurring grout cracks may point to movement. JW will review the joint and surrounding tile before recommending a cosmetic repair.

What if my tile is discontinued?

Spare stock, reclaimed pieces or a deliberately larger replacement area may be considered. JW will explain the likely visual tradeoffs before work begins.
