Interior & exterior painting

The color is visible.
The prep makes it hold.

A clean finish starts with the condition of the wall, trim, ceiling or exterior surface—not the paint can.

JW looks at what needs to be cleaned, patched, repaired, textured, sanded, masked or protected before painting begins. That lets the scope reflect the actual surface instead of hiding repair work inside a vague paint quote.

Interior roomsWalls, ceilings, doors and trim with careful protection around the space.
Exterior surfacesSiding, fascia, shutters, porches, garages and other painted structures.
Cabinets & detailsCabinet painting, doors, built-ins and finish details that need controlled prep.
Commercial interiorsPreparation and finish work for occupied or changing business spaces.

Repair before repainting

Fix what shows through before the final coat.

Paint does not correct damaged drywall, uneven texture or failing ceiling material.

Related restoration work can be included in the same project: sheetrock repair, wall and ceiling texture, popcorn ceiling removal, pressure washing before exterior work, and tub or tile reglazing where appropriate. The goal is a complete finished space, not a fresh color over an old problem.
