Pressure washing & preparation

The final finish depends on what it is attached to.

Cleaning and preparation are not separate from the paint or coating result.

Pressure washing can be included where an exterior surface needs cleaning before finish work. JW also handles patching and preparation steps around painting and coating projects so the final surface is not expected to hide dirt, loose material or untreated damage.

Floors, tile & urgent repairs

When a surface fails,
look past the visible edge.

Water damage, buckling, cupping and cracked tile can extend beyond the spot that first gets noticed.

JW handles water-damaged floor replacement, buckled or cupped sections, popped or cracked tile, fast move-in repairs, and tub or tile reglazing. The scope is based on what is damaged and how the repair must meet the surrounding finished surface.
