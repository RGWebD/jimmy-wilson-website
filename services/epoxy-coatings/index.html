Garage & interior coatings

The coating is the finish.
Preparation is the system.

A coating cannot perform better than the concrete or previous finish underneath it.

JW scopes coating work around the condition of the surface, the use of the space and the finish you want. Existing damage, old coating, repair areas and the need for cleaning or recoat work are considered before the new system goes down.
