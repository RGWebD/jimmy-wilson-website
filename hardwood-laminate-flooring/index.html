Scope the work before choosing shortcuts

Not every wood floor behaves the same way

Solid wood, engineered products and laminate respond differently to moisture, temperature and routine wear. The appropriate choice depends on the room and the manufacturer’s approved installation method. JW reviews the product information and the site before writing the preparation and installation scope.

Questions before an estimate

Useful answers before the site visit.

These answers are planning guidance, not a diagnosis of a surface that has not been inspected.

Is engineered hardwood different from laminate?

Yes. Engineered hardwood uses a real wood wear layer, while laminate uses a printed decorative layer over a manufactured core. Their care and installation requirements differ.

Can wood flooring be installed on a concrete slab?

Some products and methods are approved for concrete, subject to the manufacturer’s requirements and site conditions. The exact product must be reviewed before the scope is set.

Does laminate need an underlayment?

That depends on the product and installation method; some include an attached pad. JW follows the written requirements for the selected flooring.

Can existing hardwood be refinished instead of replaced?

Some floors can be refinished, depending on their construction, remaining wear layer and condition. An on-site review is needed before recommending refinishing.
