Read beyond the average

What to look for in contractor feedback

Read beyond the star average. Look for details about communication, surface preparation, schedule handling, cleanliness and how concerns were resolved. Then compare those themes with the written scope for your own project.

A curated page is more useful than copying every review

This site should show a small representative set of accurately attributed excerpts, with a direct link to Google for the full record. It should not present edited wording as a quotation or imply that one customer’s result is guaranteed for another project.
